Changes in this repository:
The PCG RXS-M-XS integer hash was ported/adapted for HLSL and C#, integrated
into the games' shared math/shader helpers and exposed under project-specific
names including AbPcg, RingPcg, ThPcg, BwPcg and NocMath.Pcg. Related CPU/GPU
variants were added for the projects.

The specific GPU hash is also attributed in source to Mark Jarzynski and
Marc Olano, "Hash Functions for GPU Rendering", Journal of Computer Graphics
Techniques (JCGT), volume 9, number 3, 2020:
https://www.jcgt.org/published/0009/03/02/
