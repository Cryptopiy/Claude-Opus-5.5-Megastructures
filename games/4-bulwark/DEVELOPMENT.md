## Architecture
Phase 2: the Thread framework (with Abyss and The Ring pieces) is imported and adapted per Part 3.1: copy list S1-S13 of
`docs/IMPORT_MANIFEST.md` with D-01..D-10. `docs/FRAMEWORK.md` is the map: what came from where, renames, the execution
order of one frame, how to add a beat, every command. The path (`BwPath`, `FilletPath`), `BulwarkConst` and
`BulwarkPalette` are the path builder's.

**Graybox (Phase 2 integration).** One builder, `Assets/_Game/Beats/Graybox/GrayboxPath.cs` (`IBeatBuilder`,
`IWorldUpdater`, `IKeyShotSubjects`, name `Graybox_Path`), lays the whole 1500 m walk as clean volumes, everything from
`BwPath` and `BulwarkConst`: fixed dam pieces for beats 1-4 (road deck, sea and lee faces, parapet, lee rail, the beat 1
shell with the door wall at z 120 and two sliding leaves, the gate tower with portal and atrium, a sea plane at y -8, an
inward 9 km sky sphere that the fog lights, no shadows) and lane-driven pieces from s 934 (floor strips on the exact
`FloorY` with all 270 treads and risers and the gallery ramp, walls 0.40 m outside `BwPath.Lane`, ceilings from the 14.12
heights, the beat 6 rail / trench / glass stand-in at axis + 6.0, the ledge rail, the lantern post). No colliders:
`PathGuide` generates the corridor from the same lane function (1502 colliders, 58773 coverage rays, 0 misses). The
door gap is keyed to progress (see Blockers). Beat builders of Phase 4 replace these volumes beat by beat (delete the
matching part of `GrayboxPath` when a beat lands).
