# FEATURE MATRIX — Bulwark (game 4)

Beat-by-beat status with honest notes (MASTER_PROMPT Part 12; fallback tiers of Part 13 and 14.8, ADDENDUM A1 1.10 and 6.2).

**Snapshot.** This file describes the final master of 2026-11-20: the fix wave (9 builders, `Logs/fix_x2_reports.json`)
and the lead's integration round are merged (commit 86e5d47). The final lead check ran on it at 19:00-19:03 local. The
final autotests, captures and performance runs followed on the same build (commits 1a1407e, a29e2da, 145a648). One
six-lens critic panel was run on the merged game *before* the fix wave (`Logs/panel_x2.json`). A second full critic round
was **not** run, because the user's speed order allowed one panel and one fix wave. So a fix listed here is backed by a
builder's or the lead's measurement, not by a second panel.

**Build facts.** Product `Bulwark`; Unity 6000.6.0f1, URP 17.6.0, Direct3D 11. Player `Builds/Bulwark/Bulwark.exe`
(231 MB, 0 build errors). Test machine (autotest env): NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5070 (11944 MB), AMD Ryzen 7 5700X3D. The kraken
is built from the user's two Tripo3D models (`3D Models/SM_Creature_Octopus.glb`, `3D Models/SM_Creature_MonsterTentacles.glb`).
This is a user-directed exception to "no external assets", logged in DEVELOPMENT.md (row 2026-11-19 Wave A1). The models
stay outside `Assets/`, and `Tools/kraken/**` regenerates every game asset from them.

**Tier key.** *Full* = as specified (Part 14 as amended by ADDENDUM A1 and its section 00 lead decisions). *Simplified* =
fewer details, same composition and scale. *Graybox* = clean volumes with correct light and fog. *Not built* = cut or
never implemented, with the reason. No beat is on the Graybox tier: every beat builder replaced its graybox parts. One
graybox remnant may remain in the far view. The KS28 frame shows a peach trapezoid at the crest that no ending, beat-7 or
far-field renderer draws. It may be the GrayboxDam plume cone or the beat-3 spillway sheet; its owner is not identified.

**Sources.** STATE.md; DEVELOPMENT.md (rulings R1-R6 and the final lead rows); Logs/panel_x2.json; Logs/fix_x2_reports.json;
Logs/lead_final.out; QA/tests/editmode_final.xml; QA/reports/autotest_cinematic.json, autotest_walk.json, soak.json,
