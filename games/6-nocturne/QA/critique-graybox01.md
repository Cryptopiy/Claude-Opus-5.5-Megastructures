## Verdict
Place, scale and order hold: every beat stands at its 14.4 coordinates, the whole path is floored at y 0 (Q1IntegrationTests:
396 197 floor rays, 0 holes, 0 steps in the lane; 9 000 body / camera stations, 0 hits), the walk completes at both speeds
with exact trigger / stop times. No hole, seam, z-fighting, backface, missing shader, far-plane cut or backdrop edge was found
in any capture. What blocks a clean gate is design: the upper tube and the open garden notch (agreements 2 / 5 vs 14.12 /
14.5.6), the KS10 / KS13 / KS19 compositions, and the beat-1 / KS13 / KS19 exposure.

## Phase 4 art-direction observations (not graybox defects)
The colour and texture observations below are superseded in direction by D-027 (Look v2: dark neutral palette, CC0 textures,
docs/LOOK_V2.md, QA/critique-lookv2-baseline.md); they are kept as the graybox baseline.
- Facades and Gulf faces read as one uniform confetti mosaic of cyan / magenta / amber cells at every distance (KS03-KS12): no
  strata hierarchy, no dark floors, no depth layering; the faces lack the FaceRib relief and section interiors (C05).
- The sky is one saturated magenta field (STATE); the cloud deck has no structure in KS04 / KS05 / KS18.
- The koi is a flat, very bright crescent with a plain veil (G4-08): fins, patches, scales and scanlines are Phase 4.
- Crowns are smooth blobs with straight rod branches and dark specks where limbs cross (G3-07 / G3-09).
- Wet floors show large camouflage puddle blots everywhere (GB-10) and few sign reflections; the boulevard floor is half the
  frame at pitch 0 and very dark (G1-09).
- KS09 / KS10 / KS12 read as "purple void between two walls": the abyss needs the haze sheets, traffic layers and the glowing
  lower city for depth (H3).
- Human-scale cues work in the alley and on the bridge (doors, rails, lamps); the tongue plaza and the Heart plaza are empty
  sheets at graybox.
