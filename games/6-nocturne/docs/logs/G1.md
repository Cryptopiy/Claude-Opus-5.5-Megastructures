## Design (step 1)
Code layout (one builder per hero object, dimensions as named constants with the spec section, parts sink for tests):

Decisions taken while designing (with the arithmetic; proposed for DEVELOPMENT.md in the final report):
- Alley walls = lane + 0.35 m (the lanes of NocLanes are the one source of the 14.5.1 profile; their knots use smootherstep, 14.5.1
  says smoothstep): clear width = 2 lane + 0.70 = 4.4 / 6.0 / 4.6 / 5.0 m exactly, the PathGuide collider 3 cm behind the visible wall.
- Vending machines: a front at x 2.2 would stand inside the clear width where the wall narrows (s 62: wall 2.52) and the camera
  sphere (lane 2.17 + 0.2 = 2.37) would enter it; fronts 5 mm behind the wall line at each machine's south edge (2.53 / 2.34 / 2.31).
- Noodle bar counter front 3 cm behind the wall plane x -3.0 (in the opening), so the capsule (reach lane + 0.35 = 3.0) never enters
  it; its projection still gives the KS01 counter y -0.50..-0.22.
- G1-D1 (KS02 vs slabs 0/1): south slabs 0 (x -33..-3) and 1 (3..33) with depth >= 30 m would stand at z 86/90..120, 2 m north of
  the KS02 eye (0, 1.7, 88). Their slot faces x +-3 rise to 560 m inside the slit: a ray at frame y +0.19 (elevation 72.6 deg)
  crosses z 90..120 at y 9..104 and its lateral offset reaches +-3 m at |x_ndc| = 0.027, so the open slit at that row shrinks from
  the podium edges +-0.097 to +-0.027 and below the zenith row (y +0.74) the frame shows two dark slot walls instead of cloud;
  acceptance (3) (slit mean luma >= 55) and the 14.5.1 KS02 description (roof edges down to the frame bottom, the catwalk as a dark
  band across a bright slit, the tower flank in the slit near y +0.6) need the setback zone x -30..34 open above the podia to the
  street. So the alley blocks keep their podia (22 / 31 m) to the street face, the alley towers (x <= -30 to z 116, x >= 34 to z 86)
  frame the gap, and the south slab rhythm starts at slab 2 (x 39). North side: all 7 slabs + a slab x -60..-39 to the hero edge.
- Podium 40 m with the arcade 8 m below (4 m deep) and the terrace setback 4 m (shafts at z 116 / 274); slab depth 30 m (shafts
  z 86..116 and 274..304). 84 columns on x = 3 + 6k (-39..213; the alley mouth falls between two bays, solid piers x 2.5..4.5).
- Median barrier in the median's outer 0.3 m (|dz| 11.70..12.00): median 24.0 m between the outer faces, inner face 0.32 m behind the
  lane wall (11.0 + 0.38); it ends at x 213.8 because the lane widens to the tongue's 12 m over s 396-404 (s 400: reach 11.85).
- 40 lamp posts taken from x 208 westward on both rows, skipping the C1 lane and the north row in the tram sight sector x 79-175:
  they run west to x -128 (the median continues into the city canyon). Wet sources only for heads with x >= -20 (all far-west heads
  project to s ~171 and would crowd the 32-source window).
- Street floor x -1 500..215.3: from the median the edge lies 1.7 / 1 575 rad = 0.06 deg below the horizon (sub-pixel), so the canyon
  floor never ends visibly; no face at the tongue seam x 215.3 (G2's deck continues).
